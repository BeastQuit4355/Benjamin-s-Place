House Rules

Quiet Hours

After 9:00 PM. Please keep noise down so everyone can rest.

Curfew

9:00 PM for everyone (tenants and visitors).

Gate

Opens around 5–6 AM. The gate does not lock at night. The 9 PM curfew is a house rule.

Smoking

Allowed only in the garage and waiting area. Prohibited everywhere else.

Furniture Rule (important)

Bedspacers may not bring wardrobes, chairs, or tables into the bedroom. Rooms already have dining tables and chairs. Living and waiting areas are fully furnished.

Cleaning

Mandatory group cleaning rotation for common areas, bedrooms, bathrooms, garage, and garbage.
